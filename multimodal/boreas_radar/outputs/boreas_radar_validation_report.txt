Static calibration direction is also checked numerically by comparing the calibrated camera/Radar world poses against their independent sensor pose-table poses; residuals follow in the summary.

Candidate extraction is a deterministic 2D local-maximum filter over the official normalized polar intensity scan, gated by valid azimuth rows and the stated intensity/range thresholds. Output candidates are NOT Radar object detections. Range is (range_bin + 0.5) * official pyboreas resolution, following the bin-center convention in radar_polar_to_cartesian; the official scan loader's near-range zeroing is retained and no scaling or unavailable sequence-specific range correction is invented.
Radar candidate beam timestamps are used individually; candidates outside the configured temporal tolerance are excluded for each camera frame. Camera/Radar scan timestamp proximity is only a pairing gate, not proof of spatial association.
Tracking boxes are loaded and time/pose-associated by the official pyboreas Camera.get_bounding_boxes path. The comparison uses their projected 2D ground-truth boxes, not YOLO output or ByteTrack tracks.
