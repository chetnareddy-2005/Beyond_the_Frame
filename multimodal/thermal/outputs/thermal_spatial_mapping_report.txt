Coordinate and calibration interpretation
------------------------------------------
RGB track boxes are stored in the 960x960 video canvas. The original RGB frame is 960x600 with centered top padding of 180 pixels; the source box is obtained by subtracting that padding and is checked against image bounds.
For a compatible calibration, the official StereoRectifier uses cam1's T_cn_cnm1 directly as R,T in cv2.stereoRectify(cameraMatrix1=cam0, cameraMatrix2=cam1); no inverse is inserted by this validation wrapper. Rectified point coordinates must be obtained through the corresponding official undistort/rectify maps, not same-coordinate transfer.
Rectification constrains epipolar rows but does not determine horizontal Thermal correspondence. A valid depth/disparity or independently measured cross-modal registration is required before a localized region can be emitted.
