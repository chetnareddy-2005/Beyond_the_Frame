All error statistics use unique ASSOCIATED UUID/Radar-scan pairs only. Duplicate label rows mapping to the same UUID/scan are collapsed only after checking that candidate index, range, azimuth, and intensity agree.
Radar candidate index is a deterministic ordinal local to one scan. It is preserved for within-scan traceability and is not a Radar-track ID; matching ordinals across scans do not imply the same Radar return.
The persistent identity anchor is the official BOREAS visual-object UUID. ASSOCIATED records geometric consistency between that UUID's timestamped 3D ground-truth box and an extracted raw Radar intensity candidate. This is an annotation-linked object identity associated with its nearest camera observation, not an independently generated visual detector/tracker ID. This is not Radar detection accuracy, Radar tracking accuracy, or Radar identity.
Radar evidence recurrence across UUID observations does not create a persistent Radar identity. The existing polar gates and candidate extraction are unchanged.
